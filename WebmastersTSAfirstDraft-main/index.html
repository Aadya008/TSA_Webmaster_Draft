MODULE 01 • MACHINE LEARNING FOUNDATIONS +250 XP

The Engine Room: Machine Learning & Transformer Architecture

Move beyond simple definitions. Understand how computers transform natural language into mathematical arrays, adjust synaptic weights, and compute predictions.

Core Mechanical Foundations:

  • Feed-Forward vs. Transformers: Traditional Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) processed language sequentially, word by word. This caused mathematical "vanishing gradients" where early sentence context was forgotten. Transformers process all tokens in parallel using Multi-Head Self-Attention, calculating dot-product similarity across all words simultaneously.
  • Loss Functions & Backpropagation: A neural network initializes with randomized weights. When it predicts the wrong token, a Loss Function (e.g., Categorical Cross-Entropy) calculates the mathematical error. Backpropagation calculates the partial derivative of this error with respect to every weight using the calculus Chain Rule, nudging weights along the negative gradient.
  • Vector Embeddings: Words are transformed into high-dimensional vectors ($512$ to $4,096$ dimensions). High-dimensional geometric proximity models semantic similarity—allowing models to compute linguistic relationships algebraically: $\vec{v}_{\text{King}} - \vec{v}_{\text{Man}} + \vec{v}_{\text{Woman}} \approx \vec{v}_{\text{Queen}}$.

MODULE 02 • APPLIED TOOLS & METHODOLOGIES +250 XP

The Academic Co-Pilot: Practical Tools & Prompt Calibration

Learn to treat generative AI not as an authoritative factual database or a ghostwriter, but as a calibrated cognitive amplifier for research and critique.

The C.R.A.F.T. Prompt Engineering Framework:

  • Context (C): Provide the historical, scientific, or literary background. Constrain the scope to prevent irrelevant drift.
  • Role (R): Assign an explicit professional persona (e.g., "Act as an AP Environmental Science research evaluator...") to force specialized vocabulary and reasoning.
  • Action (A): Command explicit cognitive tasks (e.g., "Contrast," "Critique line of reasoning," "Isolate logical fallacies") rather than generic prompts like "write about."
  • Format (F): Specify structural output requirements (e.g., comparative Markdown tables, JSON arrays, numbered proofs).
  • Target Audience (T): Calibrate the technical depth and comprehension level (e.g., "Tailor explanation for a 10th-grade peer reviewer").

Mitigating Stochastic Hallucinations:

Large Language Models are probabilistic next-token predictors, not knowledge engines. They generate text that statistically sounds authentic, frequently inventing non-existent academic papers, author names, and DOIs. Scholars must maintain rigorous verification protocols by validating claims through primary indexes like PubMed, JSTOR, and Google Scholar.

MODULE 03 • ETHICAL GOVERNANCE & INTEGRITY +250 XP

The Ethical Compass: Algorithmic Bias, Privacy, & Honor Codes

Evaluate the statistical roots of algorithmic discrimination, statutory privacy mandates, and the boundary between permissible assistance and academic plagiarism.

Algorithmic Disparity & Statutory Protections:

  • Training Corpus Representation Skew: Algorithms do not possess moral animus; they extract empirical patterns from historical training text. When training data contains cultural imbalances or underrepresents demographic groups, the model's confidence distribution flattens, resulting in elevated false rejection rates for those groups.
  • The Four-Fifths Rule (Disparate Impact): Under federal compliance guidelines (EEOC Uniform Guidelines), an automated selection process has adverse disparate impact if the selection rate for a protected demographic is less than $80\%$ ($\frac{4}{5}$) of the selection rate of the highest-scoring cohort.
  • Institutional Data Privacy (FERPA): Commercial public AI endpoints frequently log user prompt submissions for downstream fine-tuning. Inputting student PII (Personally Identifiable Information), disciplinary records, or unredacted work into public chats violates federal FERPA safeguards.
